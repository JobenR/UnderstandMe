Privacy

How your information is handled.

A plain-language description of how the site works today.

The short version

UnderstandMe has no accounts for patients and no server that receives what you enter into an exercise. Your answers stay in your browser unless you print, save, copy, or email them yourself.

What stays in your browser

  • Exercise answers. Held in the page while you work. Some tools can keep a local copy so you can come back: Values Card Sort resumes an in-progress sort, and Step Builder and Week Builder offer an opt-in save. These live only in that browser on that device and can be deleted inside the tool or by clearing your browser data.
  • Provider profile. If a provider chooses “Save on this device”, their name, practice, and contact details are stored in that browser. They can remove it at any time.

Links from a provider

A link a provider shares contains that provider’s profile details inside the web address, so printouts can carry their name. Anyone who has the link can read those details, and they may appear in browser history. The link never contains anything a patient enters, so providers should not put sensitive information in their profile or note.

When you print, copy, or email

Printing, downloading, and copying happen on your device. “Email to my provider” opens a draft in your own email app, which you review before sending. Email is not encrypted by default, so consider what you include.

Third parties and logs

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Whoever hosts this site may keep standard access logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

Not a medical record

Nothing here is a clinical record, assessment, or diagnosis. These tools are educational and do not replace professional care.

This page describes the site’s current behavior and is a working draft. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch, and updated if accounts or data storage are added.
