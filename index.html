How it works

From a link to a conversation

Three quiet steps, with nothing to install and nothing to log into.

1

Choose

A provider picks one exercise or builds a short packet. Anyone can also explore on their own.

2

Reflect

The exercise runs privately in the browser, one gentle step at a time, at your own pace.

3

Bring it along

Print, save as PDF, copy as text, or email a draft. Then talk it through in session.

Calm by design

Built to feel safe to use

Reflective work should not feel like filling out a form for someone else.

Private by default

There is no patient database. What you type stays in your browser unless you choose to print, save, or send it.

Paced, not pressured

One idea per screen, plain language, and nothing timed unless the exercise needs it.

Made to be shared

Every exercise produces a clean one-page summary designed for the conversation that follows.

Start with one small exercise.

No account, no cost, and no commitment. Pick the one that fits what is on your mind.
