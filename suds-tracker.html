Exposure & SUDS Tracker

A live stopwatch for exposure work

Built for running an exposure exercise in session: start the clock, rate distress before you begin, and this tracks the rest — prompting a check-in at whatever interval you set, logging every rating with a timestamp, and charting how it rises and (with continued exposure) comes back down.

Run more than one exposure in the same sitting and they're automatically compared side by side, so change across the session — not just within one exposure — is visible immediately.

Session data — exposures, ratings, timestamps — lives only in this browser tab. Nothing about a client is saved or sent anywhere, and closing or reloading the tab clears it, so print or save the summary before you do.

A session-documentation aid for clinicians, not a standardized or diagnostic instrument. It doesn’t replace clinical judgment.

If you or someone you're with is in crisis, call or text 988 — the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7 in the US.
