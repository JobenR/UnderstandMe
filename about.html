About

Tools that help people understand themselves, and each other.

UnderstandMe is a small, focused toolkit that makes reflective exercises easier to do, easier to keep, and easier to talk about.

Private by design

We do not run a patient database. Exercises work in your browser, and you decide what leaves it.

Calm by design

Soft visuals, plain language, and a steady pace. Reflection should not feel like a test.

Useful in the room

Every exercise ends in a clear summary that gives provider and patient something concrete to discuss.

Where it came from

These exercises began as worksheets and small tools used between sessions in a private psychotherapy practice. Clients found them easier to engage with when they were interactive, private, and finished with something they could bring back. UnderstandMe brings the same tools to any provider and any patient.

Who it is for

Patients

Use any exercise on your own or when a provider sends you a link. You do not need an account, and nothing you enter is sent to us.

Providers

Add your details so printouts carry your name, build guided packets, and share them with a link. Accounts and sign-in across devices are being developed.

What it is not

UnderstandMe offers educational, self-reflection tools. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care, and using it does not create a provider–patient relationship. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or call 911 in an emergency.
